## Gates

- Gate 0 — pass. The page renders with one main landmark and no missing styles, failed resources, console errors, body overflow, or text-contrast failures at 1440, 768, and 390 pixels.
- Gate 1 — pass. The hero names the audience tension and distinction; the public-work questions and project descriptions provide credible proof; every action states its destination.
- Gate 1.5 — pass. Entry begins at the product argument, the in-page actions land on named sections, external links provide the success destination, and normal browser navigation supplies recovery.

## Marketing mode

- Product argument — 2/2. The first viewport distinguishes a connected software factory from generic AI output and offers two concrete routes.
- Honest proof — 2/2. Project capabilities and public destinations come from current public repositories and live sites. No customers, usage metrics, testimonials, or urgency claims are invented.
- Structural fingerprint — 2/2. The manifesto → public proving grounds → open-source ledger → building principles sequence follows the work’s argument rather than a stock conversion template.
- Coherent identity — 2/2. Editorial type, paper and ink, signal orange, rules, numbering, and the system loop form one design vocabulary.
- Purposeful expression — 2/2. The only animated figure explains how the projects feed one another; reduced motion turns it static.
- Action and mobile — 2/2. Calls to action name their destinations. The 390-pixel layout changes navigation, figure width, experiments, ledger rows, and principles into task-appropriate narrow compositions.
